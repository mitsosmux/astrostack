AstroStack Privacy Policy

Updated: 5 October 2026

Applies to AstroStack for Android, version 1.0.10.

Support and privacy contact: mitsosmux@gmail.com

1. Processing on your device

AstroStack processes the photographs you select on your Android device. The app has no Internet permission and does not operate a developer upload service. It contains no advertisements, analytics SDKs, automatic crash-report uploads or app account system. Your photos and saved stacks are not sent to the developer by the app.

2. Information the app accesses

You select input files and export destinations through Android's document picker. AstroStack reads the selected image pixels and image metadata needed to validate and process those files, such as dimensions, sensor layout and exposure information. It does not request broad access to your photo library, your contacts, microphone or device location. It may retain permission to reopen selected files when the document provider allows this.

Optional Capture frames requests camera permission while you use Capture. AstroStack saves captured Bayer RAW originals in private device storage, grouped by session and Light, Dark or Flat batch. Leaving Capture stops the sequence; completed frames remain saved. Review previews are generated locally and do not upload or replace RAW originals.

3. Local storage and security

The app stores selected file references, processing settings, saved stacks, thumbnails and editing sessions in its private Android app storage. Temporary files are used during processing. Android's app sandbox restricts access to private app storage; AstroStack disables Android backup of this private app data. This is not a promise of protection against an unlocked or compromised device. Exported files are stored outside the private app area at the location you choose.

4. Exporting and other providers

AstroStack writes an export only to a destination you select. A cloud-backed document provider may download selected inputs or upload your exported files under its own privacy policy. Other apps or people can access an export according to the permissions of that destination. Google Play and Android services operate under their own policies; purchasing AstroStack is handled by Google Play, not by an in-app payment system operated by the developer.

5. Retention and deletion

Saved stacks and their editing sessions remain on your device until you delete the saved project, clear AstroStack's app storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Selected file references and preferences are separate from saved projects: removing a project does not necessarily clear these preferences. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes private app data. Deleting a saved project does not delete your original input photos or exports; delete those using your file manager or document provider. Temporary job files are cleaned after completion or cancellation; stale interrupted-job files are cleaned when the app is started again. Captured RAW originals remain until you explicitly discard a reviewed batch, delete its captured session, clear app storage or uninstall. Deleting a saved stack does not remove these captured originals. Export original DNGs creates an independent copy at the destination you choose. There is no AstroStack account to delete.

6. Support messages

Contacting support opens your email app. Only information you choose to send is included in your message. Support correspondence is used to answer your request and resolve the issue, and is retained only as needed for support or legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing mitsosmux@gmail.com. Your email provider and the support mailbox provider handle email under their own policies. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information or photographs.

7. Policy changes and questions

The policy will be updated when the app's data handling changes. The date above identifies this version. For questions about privacy, contact mitsosmux@gmail.com.

8. This policy website

This policy is hosted by GitHub Pages. The page has no analytics, advertising scripts or remote fonts. GitHub may process website request information under its own privacy policy; this is separate from AstroStack, which has no Internet permission.
