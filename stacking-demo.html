Real phone stacking demonstration

A continuous recording of AstroStack processing real Milky Way photographs on an Android phone.

Open or download the MP4 recording · 2 minutes 15 seconds

What the recording shows

  1. Select real RAW light frames and dark/flat calibration files through Android’s document picker.
  2. Use Stable registration and start the stack. Processing progress changes as calibration, alignment and stacking proceed.
  3. Open Android’s notification panel and see the ongoing AstroStack processing notification.
  4. Open the completed Milky Way stack, adjust light settings, place background samples, rotate the image and export a TIFF.

Recorded by the developer on 3 October 2026. The full sequence is retained, with no cuts or speed changes. The public copy is compressed for streaming, with audio removed and unrelated notification text and connection names covered. The original recording is preserved locally. This video supports the Google Play foreground-service declaration; it is not a claim of compatibility with every camera or device.
